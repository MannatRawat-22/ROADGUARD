# 🚨 ROADGUARD — AI-Powered Road Safety & Emergency Response

> **Detect. Alert. Protect.**

ROADGUARD is a smart road-safety application designed to help users respond quickly to accidents, road hazards, low-visibility conditions, and unsafe areas.

The system combines **accident detection, emergency alerts, hazard reporting, and real-time safety information** into one simple interface.

## 🌐 Live Demo

**[ROADGUARD — Live Website](https://mannatrawat-22.github.io/ROADGUARD/)**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Road accidents and road hazards can become more dangerous when help is delayed.

Drivers and passengers may encounter:

* 🚗 Road accidents
* 🕳️ Potholes and damaged roads
* 🌫️ Low-visibility conditions
* ⚠️ Unsafe or accident-prone areas
* 🚧 Unexpected road hazards
* 📵 Delayed emergency communication

ROADGUARD aims to reduce response time by providing immediate safety assistance and connecting users with emergency contacts.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ROADGUARD provides a centralized safety dashboard where users can:

* Detect potential accidents
* Trigger emergency assistance
* Send alerts to emergency contacts
* Report road hazards
* Identify unsafe areas
* Receive low-visibility road warnings
* Access important emergency information

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚨 Accident Detection

Detects potential accident situations and provides an emergency response option.

### 📱 Shake-to-Alert

A shake gesture can be used as a quick emergency trigger when the user cannot easily interact with the screen.

### 📞 Emergency Response

Provides quick access to emergency contacts and calling options during critical situations.

### 🕳️ Road Hazard Reporting

Users can report hazards such as:

* Potholes
* Broken roads
* Road debris
* Construction zones
* Other dangerous obstacles

### 🌫️ Low-Visibility Alerts

Provides warnings for potentially dangerous visibility conditions such as fog or poor visibility.

### ⚠️ Unsafe Area Reporting

Users can identify and report locations where they feel road conditions or surroundings may be unsafe.

### 🗺️ Safety Dashboard

A centralized interface provides important road-safety information and emergency actions.
